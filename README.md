# Ultimate-Cyber-Login-Battle
Ultimate Cyber Login Battle is a chaotic, parody login experience where users fight fake malware, survive pop‑ups, and defeat the Ransomware Dragon—all before being denied access anyway. A fun cyber‑security mini‑game built with HTML, CSS, JS, and pure hackathon energy.

🐉 Ultimate Cyber Login Battle
A chaotic, hilarious cyber‑security themed login experience.
Welcome to Ultimate Cyber Login Battle — the only login screen where you don’t just enter your password…
You fight malware, survive fake cyber‑attacks, and defeat the mighty Ransomware Dragon to earn your access (which still fails anyway 💀).
This project is built for fun, hackathons, cyber‑security demos, and anyone who’s suffered from too many CAPTCHAs, MFA prompts, or poorly designed login portals.

🚀 Features
🔹 Phase 1: The Fake Malware Gauntlet
Click to defeat waves of “⚠️ Malware Detected” popups.
Your firewall health slowly drops as you survive the chaos.
🔹 Phase 2: The Unexpected Plot Twist
Just when it seems safe, the Ransomware Dragon appears:

🐉 "You haven't won just yet!"

With screen shaking, red flashing popups, and heavier attacks, the boss fight begins.
🔹 Phase 3: Boss Fight – Ransomware Dragon

Animated matrix hacking background
Giant emotional support cyber‑dragon
Encryption attacks
Click‑based combat
Health bars for both you and the boss

🔹 Final Joke
Even after defeating everything:

Login Failed: Session Timeout. Please try again.

And the page reloads.
Perfectly annoying. 😂
